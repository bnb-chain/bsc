01Executive Summary & Vision

CoinBosa is building a unified global ecosystem merging sovereign Blockchain Infrastructure, Orchestration Artificial Intelligence, borderless FinTech Solutions and Academic Training. Founded by Coinbosa, Inc. (Delaware, USA), the project solves the critical problem of fragmentation between decentralized finance (DeFi), everyday cryptocurrency use and universal access to advanced AI technologies.

Powered by its native coin BOSA (fixed supply sealed at 700,000,000 units), the CoinBosa ecosystem guarantees instant low-fee transactions, direct access to global in-person payments through the Coinbosa Card, as well as an optimized multi-model AI platform.

03CoinBosa Omni IA Studio & AI Innovation

The CoinBosa Omni IA Studio module is an advanced artificial intelligence orchestrator solving the complexity of model selection.

Dynamic Prompt Orchestration Algorithm

For each user request R, the CoinBosa engine computes the fitness score Sm for each model m ∈ M:

Sm(R) = wp · P(m | domain) + wt · 1Latencym + wc · Accuracym

The AI selects the optimal model m* = arg max Sm(R), generates the workflow and dispatches the instructions to the specialized agents (Code, Image, Text, Video).

  • V1 Live Version: Universal multi-model support (code generation, image creation, content writing, analysis).
  • V2 Upcoming Version: Launch of the proprietary CoinBosa AI model trained on financial and software engineering data.

04FinTech Ecosystem & Global Neobank

4.1 Coinbosa Card: A Global Solution Without Geographic Limits

Conceived as early as 2022, the Coinbosa Card solves the major problem of cryptocurrency holders unable to make everyday purchases or to obtain cash immediately while traveling internationally. Unlike competing cards (such as Trust Wallet) restricted to Europe or the United States, the Coinbosa Card is deployed worldwide with no regional limitation.

4.2 Sovereign Neobank Platform

CoinBosa is progressively deploying a Neobank infrastructure in strict compliance with local and international financial regulations (KYC/AML), enabling direct Crypto <-> Fiat conversion and automated bank deposits and withdrawals.

05CoinBosa Academy & Ecosystem VPN Services

  • CoinBosa Academy: A training platform that has already taught several thousand students. Online courses and regional seminars focused on FinTech, Blockchain, AI and Trading.
  • CoinBosa VPN: A proprietary virtual private network guaranteeing secure and private access to CoinBosa servers from any region of the world.
